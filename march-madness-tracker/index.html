Prediction Methodology

Scoring predictions are based on the following factors:

  • Season PPG baseline: Each player's 2025-26 regular season scoring average serves as the primary input.
  • Tournament intensity multiplier: Historical data shows elite scorers tend to see a ~3-8% bump in March Madness due to higher stakes and increased usage. We apply a multiplier of 1.03x for consistent scorers and 1.07x for high-variance players.
  • Opponent strength adjustment: Higher-seeded teams face weaker opponents early, inflating scoring in rounds 1-2. A -5% adjustment is applied for later rounds against tougher defenses.
  • Conference tournament momentum: Players who performed well in their conference tournaments (e.g., Boyd's 25 PPG in the Big Ten tourney) receive a slight upward adjustment.
  • Confidence rating: "High" indicates consistent scorers on favorably seeded teams; "Medium" indicates either high variance or lower seed (tougher path).

Note: Predictions assume each scenario's game count. Actual results depend on team advancement. Data sourced from ESPN, CBS Sports, and official athletic sites as of March 17, 2026.
