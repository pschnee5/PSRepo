Prediction Methodology

Projected tournament points are calculated based on:

  • Season PPG with a tournament intensity multiplier (~1.05x for top seeds, reduced for lower seeds facing tougher opponents)
  • Team seed and historical round-by-round win probabilities (e.g., 2-seeds win ~88% of R64, ~67% of R32, ~50% of S16)
  • Expected games = sum of win probabilities for each round the team could play
  • Players on eliminated teams are locked at their actual tournament points scored
  • Click any player card to log actual game scores as the tournament progresses
